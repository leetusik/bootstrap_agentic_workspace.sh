## Decisions

_Durable cross-slice decisions. Replace a superseded line; never stack versions._

- **Shape A (split by change), not B (dogfood).** Two middle slices: S1 = the `research` kind end
  to end, S2 = the Aside change with its investigation done inline. B would have spent half the
  4-slice budget proving the new kind and shipped change 2 as findings only, leaving the operator's
  stated objective unlanded. A ships both. The dogfood is not lost: `DECOMP` did the Aside
  investigation and handed it down through `## Notes for later slices`, which is exactly the
  mechanism the `research` kind institutes — the phase demonstrates the handoff without buying a
  slice for it.
- **This phase has no `DECOMP2`.** The operator's 4-slice cap forbids it, even though this is the
  phase generalizing the pattern. Recorded so the review does not read its absence as an omission.
- **Both middle slices are `implementation / high`.** Each edits 6-10 files and writes real logic
  plus contract prose; neither is the one-line-or-docs case that `low` is for.
- **v36 lands in S2, not S1.** `installer/main.py:38` (`WORKSPACE_VERSION = 35` -> 36) and the
  `CHANGELOG.md` `## v36` section are written once, by the last middle slice, covering both
  changes. S1 ships its half without touching the version.
- **S1 before S2, and `depends_on` says so.** They overlap in `CLAUDE.md`, both agent files,
  `tests/retrofit_smoke.sh` and the rebuilt installer, and S2's changelog entry describes S1's work.
- **`research` routes high by *kind*, not by rating.** It joins `decomposition` and `review` in the
  always-high clause everywhere routing is stated, rather than leaning on `--risk high`; the rating
  is still set `high` so the record never contradicts the routing, and the contract says plainly
  that the kind wins if they ever disagree. Routing by rating alone would let one `low` send the
  slice that decides the next cut to the mid tier.
- **`DECOMP2` is written as having two origins**, research and `build-after`, neither a special case
  of the other — not as a design device with a research exception bolted on. `build-after` is
  unchanged in every particular. A genuine third pass is `DECOMP3`; that is one extra id, not a
  numbering scheme.
- **`design-cowork` keeps its design framing** and gained one cross-reference bullet only ("the id is
  not design-only"). Generalizing the id inside the design skill would have made a design reader
  carry machinery they never use.
- **The READMEs were deliberately left alone.** They never enumerated slice kinds (`co-work` is
  absent from them too), and their tier prose stays true because a research slice is rated `high`.
- **Aside is prescribed as doctrine, not integrated as tooling.** It is macOS-only, not installed
  here, and needs an operator-run download plus an account, so S2 cannot demonstrate a live browser
  run from an executor. It writes the prescription, the surface, and the fallback. This repo has no
  product UI to verify, so nothing here regresses from that.

## Operator Questions
