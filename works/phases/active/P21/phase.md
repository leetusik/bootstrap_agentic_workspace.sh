# Phase P21: close the workspace's context leaks, measured against live adopters

_Intent: see [intent.md](intent.md)._

## Objective

Measure where the workspace actually spends context across four live adopting repos (changple5 v36, changple_web v35, Mijual v36, arb_upbit_1 v35 -- ~1,140 slices, 83 archived phases between three of them), then close only the leaks the measurement justifies. Confirmed leak with an operator-proposed remedy to research first: per-review durable-doc consolidation rewrites whole docs that only grow (changple5 backend.md at v0054, 420 KB, rewritten by every review) -- research retiring doc-new-version from the review slice in favour of dedicated operator-created docs phases, and what a review still records if so. Open questions: whether the 16 KB phase.md budget is right (post-v35 notebooks ride within bytes of the ceiling -- is mid-phase compaction losing decisions under that pressure?); whether plan.md + result.md suffice as the orchestrator's post-slice record; what the review may read instead of every result.md; executor validation cost; where keep-tests-small needs teeth (changple5: 494 test files / 6.3 MB; the other three comply). Two suspects retracted by measurement: archiving works (rotate-backlog; 56/20/7 archived) and the notebook budget is respected since v35. DECOMP cuts a research slice first -- the fix list is not knowable before the measurement -- and DECOMP2 cuts the remedies from findings. Machinery and doctrine only: adopter repos are read as evidence and receive a documented procedure, never edits from here.

## Decisions

_Durable cross-slice decisions. Replace a superseded line; never stack versions._

- Research-first cut: the fix list was not knowable before measuring, so `P21.DECOMP` created only `P21.S1` and `P21.DECOMP2`. S1 measured it; `DECOMP2` has now cut the remedies (last decision below). (P21.DECOMP, P21.S1, P21.DECOMP2)
- Scope boundary held: machinery and doctrine in **this** repo only. The four adopter repos are read-only evidence -- S1 verified them clean after measuring (changple_web's one timestamp diff is pre-existing). Anything they need ships as a procedure they run. (P21.DECOMP)
- Acceptance gate stays **waived** (`required: false`, "machinery and doctrine only"). Confirmed at `DECOMP2`: no cut remedy touches an operator-visible running product -- they change `scripts/workflow.py`, `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/*` and docs guidance. (P21.DECOMP, P21.S1, P21.DECOMP2)
- **The doc-rewrite leak is dominant; everything else is a rounding error.** Per-review consolidation is **90-97 %** of the review's read budget (97-99 % naive). changple5 has paid ~9.8 M tokens across 65 consolidations; `P80.REVIEW` (16 versions) exceeded a 1 M-token window; its `backend.md` is v0054 / 420,395 B with its last 16 versions averaging **2.7 % new lines**. (P21.S1 §2)
- **The remedy is to defer *when* consolidation runs, not to change *what* a doc version is.** Consolidation routinely supersedes wrong lines in place (`P88`/`P90` review results), so an append-only delta format would trade a size problem for a correctness problem. Sectioning cuts the read to 10-18 % in changple5 but **83-143 %** in the three small-doc repos -- a secondary remedy for oversized sections only, never the substitute. (P21.S1 §2.4, §2.7)
- **Deferral is a generalisation, not an invention: parallel mode has run it since v24** -- `execution.consolidation: pending|done`, the `parallel-merge-finish` listing, `parallel-consolidated <P>`, the `_phase_blockers` archiving guard. Lift them out of the `execution` block and they serve every phase. (P21.S1 §11c)
- **Deferral buys two savings beyond the review's critical path:** batching every 5 phases yields **3.3x fewer doc versions** in changple5 (368 -> 111), and re-review rounds stop re-consolidating -- those duplicates are **11 % of changple5's and 23 % of Mijual's** doc versions today. (P21.S1 §2.6)
- **Two couplings any remedy must handle.** (1) The gate writes through the retired path: stage 4 appends `## Regression Checklist` rows and `## Operator Runtime` is the browser manifest -- both cheap (~13 k tok worst case vs 74-734 k saved), so the review keeps a **narrow named write to exactly those two**. (2) `docs/current` staleness becomes operator-paced, so the consolidation debt must be **visible** in `next` / `validate`. (P21.S1 §11d)
- **`plan.md` + `result.md` suffice -- do not add a third file.** 9 ad-hoc third files across 1,137 slices (0.8 %; the 24 `brief.md`/`review.md` are legacy). Verdict-block-first is **63/63** in phases created on/after 2026-08-29 and **0/1,074** before. The one real `audit.md` (changple5 P87, 53 KB) is the pressure the v36 `research` kind already answers. (P21.S1 §4)
- **Mid-phase compaction is not losing decisions.** The 3 tightest post-budget notebooks, audited across 49 revisions: every apparent drop was a reword, an explicitly-spent decision, or one already landed in `result.md` + `docs/current`. Key on the decision **id**, never the line text -- line-diffing gives 9-18 false drops per revision. (P21.S1 §3.3)
- **Executor validation is a wall-clock cost, not a context leak.** 43 % of slices run a whole suite (up to 1,393 tests), but the recorded validation block is a median **1,032 B**. Trimming it would buy safety loss for no tokens. (P21.S1 §6)
- **The notebook budget works, but its shape is wrong.** Every phase created on/after 2026-08-29 is under budget; the pre-budget over-runs stay retracted. But the **byte ceiling binds and the line ceiling never does** (92 % of 16,384 B occupied vs 69 % of 200 lines), and **37 % of the ceiling is content the slice may not compress** -- generated `## Slices` (18.9 %) plus append-only `## Doc impact` (18.1 %). Deferring consolidation makes the second worse. (P21.S1 §3.2)
- **keep-tests-small needs teeth in exactly one place: changple5** (test:source **0.42** vs 0.11-0.13 in the other three). Post-rule mean file 12,561 B vs pre-workspace 13,113 B, and **40 post-rule files exceed 20 KB** -- but 218 files / 2.86 MB predate the workspace, so half the sprawl is inherited culture and this is only weakly a *context* leak. The `test:source` ratio is a cleaner lever than a per-file cap. (P21.S1 §7)
- **A leak the intent did not name: the contract itself.** `CLAUDE.md` + the executor agent file are **~16.3 k tokens read on every dispatch**, up **5x in two months** (21,711 B on 2026-07-02 -> 65,233 B at v37). Same shape as the doc leak, but the contract's completeness is load-bearing, so acting on it is an operator call (OQ2) -- `DECOMP2` deliberately cut no slice for it. (P21.S1 §6, P21.DECOMP2)
- **The cut: four remedy slices, R1+R2 merged into one.** `S2` = R1 (defer consolidation) **plus R2** (the review's narrow named write to `## Regression Checklist` / `## Operator Runtime`), because R2's file set is a **subset** of R1's -- merging satisfies "R2 with or before R1" structurally rather than by ordering discipline, keeps one coherent doctrine change instead of an incoherent intermediate in a self-hosting repo, and costs no extra blast radius. `S3` (R3) and `S4` (R4) follow on S2's generalised state shape; `S5` (R5) is independent. All four are `high`: each edits `scripts/workflow.py`, `CLAUDE.md` or `.claude/*` and triggers the installer rebuild, so none is a one-line or docs-only edit. (P21.DECOMP2)

## Operator Questions

_Questions only the operator can answer. Append only; every entry is routed at the review -- folded into the acceptance walkthrough or filed with `defer-job`. An unrouted entry is a review finding._

- **OQ1 -- how often will you actually run a docs phase?** The remedy makes `docs/current` staleness operator-paced. At every 5 phases the batching win is 3.3x; at every 10 it is 5.9x, but changple5 would then carry ~10 phases of unconsolidated `## Doc impact` notes, and those notes are read by every slice out of a 16 KB notebook. A stated target cadence lets `DECOMP2` size the debt-visibility remedy (R3). (P21.S1)
- **OQ2 -- `CLAUDE.md` + agent files are ~16.3 k tokens on every dispatch and grew 5x in two months. Act, or accept?** Cutting them trades tokens for behaviour, and the contract's completeness is load-bearing, so this is not a measurement call. If "act", `DECOMP2` can cut a compaction slice; if "accept", say so and it stays a recorded number. (P21.S1 §6)
- **OQ3 -- should the notebook budget become bytes-only, and should it be raised?** Lines never bind (69 % occupancy vs 92 % of bytes). Separately, 37 % of the ceiling is content the slice cannot compress, and deferring doc consolidation grows the append-only `## Doc impact` share. Options: raise the byte ceiling, exclude the generated `## Slices` block from the count, or move `## Doc impact` out of the notebook. (P21.S1 §3.2)
- **OQ4 -- changple5's test sprawl is in an adopter repo this phase may not edit.** The measurement says teeth are needed there and only there. Do you want `DECOMP2` to ship a documented procedure / an advisory `validate` warning that changple5 runs itself, or to leave the rule as-is and treat 0.42 as that repo's inherited baseline? (P21.S1 §7)
