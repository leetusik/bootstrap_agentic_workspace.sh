### The open judgment call — `CLAUDE.md` at 34,419 B (+1,036, +3.1 %)

**Accepted, no fix slice.** Four reasons, in order of weight:

1. **The cap was S4's plan constraint, never an intent requirement.** `intent.md` says the opposite:
   clarification 1 puts the 33 KB → ≤ 12 KB slimming in **D8**, "a dedicated editorial phase, not
   folded into other work". A phase whose own intent defers the trim cannot fail for not doing it.
2. **The premise behind the cap was measurably wrong, and S4 proved it rather than assumed it.** The
   plan expected the rewritten Read Order to be *shorter*; it is **+173 B**, because the old four lines
   were bare pointers while the new three carry the discipline (just in time, sections not documents,
   the two never-reads). The per-touch-point table in `P18.S4/result.md` shows three compression passes
   already spent, including a genuine **−90 B** from de-duplicating the *Slice context* line.
3. **Closing the last ~700 B means half-doing D8.** The largest true duplication left — the
   executor/orchestrator division stated in both *Driving This Workspace* and *Hard Rules* — is exactly
   D8's material. A fix slice here would either delete rules the plan froze or pre-empt the editorial
   phase badly.
4. **The arithmetic is not close.** +1,036 B of prefix (~260 tokens) per dispatch against dropping a
   330 KB doc-set read and a 54 KB index from *every* dispatch and capping the notebook at 16 KB where
   P15 reached 819 lines. Adding the executor body's +3.2 KB, the phase still buys back two orders of
   magnitude more than it spends.
